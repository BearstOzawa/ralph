# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project expects respectful, direct, and technically grounded collaboration.

Examples of acceptable behavior:

- discussing code and architecture in good faith
- giving specific, actionable review feedback
- challenging ideas without attacking people
- documenting assumptions clearly

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, threats, or discriminatory language
- personal attacks or bad-faith argument
- deliberately disruptive behavior
- publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation for behavior that harms the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

For serious conduct issues, contact the repository maintainer through a private channel used for repository administration.
